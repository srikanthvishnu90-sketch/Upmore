Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

The short version. We collect the minimum needed to run your account and your money plan: your name, email, onboarding answers, plan progress, things you save, and your Guide chats. We never sell your data. No ads, no third-party trackers. Your chat messages are processed by our AI provider to answer you. You can ask us to export or delete everything at any time.

What we collect. Account info: name and email, from Google sign-in or email + password (passwords are hashed — we never see them). Onboarding answers: name, US state, hours per week, paycheck status, cash you can park. Your activity: plan progress, subscriptions / receipts / ledger entries / renewals / claims you enter, Guide chat messages, payout proof you submit, reminders, notification preferences.

What we don't collect. No payment details, no bank logins, no location tracking, no contacts, no ad IDs. Bank and Gmail connections aren't live yet — when they are, they'll be read-only, optional, and this policy will be updated first.

Who processes your data. Supabase (sign-in + database, US — your rows are isolated to you), Vercel (hosts the app), Anthropic (your Guide messages go to Claude so it can answer; chats aren't used for training), Google (sign-in only, if you choose it). Nobody else. We never sell your data.

Cookies & tracking. Your sign-in session lives in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. No third-party analytics or ad trackers.

How long we keep it. While your account is active. Ask us to delete your account and we delete your profile, chats, progress, and saved items.

Your rights. Ask us any time to see, correct, export, or delete your data: support@upmore.app.

Children. Upmore isn't for under-13s.

Security. Everything moves over encrypted connections; database access is restricted per user. If we ever learn of a breach affecting your data, we'll tell you.

Terms of Service (plain English). Upmore is a guide, not a guarantee — payouts come from third-party companies with their own terms. You're responsible for your own accounts and actions; never share passwords with anyone, including us. Don't use Upmore for fraud or fake accounts. Upmore is in beta and things may change; we may suspend accounts that abuse the service.
