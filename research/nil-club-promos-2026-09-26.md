# NIL Club promos — research 2026-09-26

Source for 8 new Upmore routes (R9323–R9330), category "NIL Athlete Deals".

## What NIL Club is
- Team-based NIL platform operated by YOKE (founded 2020 by two college athletes + a fan).
- Scale (PR Newswire, 2026-03-04): 20,000+ active team groups, 2,000+ schools, 650,000 student-athletes. Largest agent-free NIL platform.
- App: iOS/Android ("NIL Club"), ~4.5★. Sign up at nilclub.com/app.

## Core mechanic: team fan-subscription revenue share
- Fans pay monthly membership ($5/mo minimum, pay-what-you-can up to ~$20/mo) for exclusive athlete content (training clips, behind-the-scenes, game-day).
- Revenue split EQUALLY among every athlete on the roster — walk-on gets the same share as the star.
- Athletes post at their own pace; payouts via PayPal or Venmo. Under-18 needs parental consent.
- Legal review per state before operating; compliance tooling adjusts to athlete's state/school rules.

## Brand Deals tab (launched 2025-08-19)
- In-app marketplace: brands post campaigns; athletes browse/filter (sport, location, deal type), opt in, apply with a short pitch, complete deliverables, get paid.
- First 90 days: 50,000+ students completed ≥1 brand deal; 100,000+ verified actions (clicks/sales). Tracking via Impact.com / Awin.
- Deal types per NIL Club's athlete guide:
  1. **Direct-to-Athlete** — sign up for products/services you genuinely use; earn commission per verified conversion. Examples: banking apps, subscriptions, consumer products.
  2. **Peer-to-Peer content** — create content featuring a brand (unboxing, day-in-the-life, product features); paid per post or per campaign.
  3. **Product seeding** — free products (apparel, equipment, nutrition, personal care); may or may not require content.
  4. Also offered: referral commissions, flat-fee deals, discounted access, exclusive experiences.
- Compliance: disclosure/reporting handled in-app; FTC sponsorship disclosure required (#ad); deals disclosed to school per rules; no pay-for-play.

## Documented brand campaigns (nilclub.com/resources, PR)
- SoFi (Direct): 59% funding rate (4.8x industry avg), 47,000+ new accounts.
- Amazon Prime Student (Peer-to-Peer): 17.4K Prime Student signups via athlete referrals, $658K revenue.
- Subway (Peer-to-Peer content): 174 athletes, 183 content pieces, 1.1M impressions in 21 days.
- Coinbase (Direct): 46% conversion, 4.1K depositors.
- LaserAway: 78.6% book rate, 1,894 consultations, $60K+ revenue.
- Tempo: 2.2K new customers, $824K estimated added LTV.
- Comfrt: athletes picked hoodie+sweatpants, posted an Instagram Reel from a real moment; got apparel reimbursement + 10% commission per sale; 2.3x expected participation.
- Also named: Uber Eats, Instacart, Gatorade, Ulta Beauty, Acorns, Revolut, LoveShackFancy.
- 4M+ verified conversions facilitated overall.

## Athlete earnings (NIL Club's own guide)
- Most athletes: $1K–$10K/year through many smaller deals. Stars: $100K+.
- NIL earnings are taxable. Deals over $600 typically must be disclosed to the school.

## Eligibility (honest requirements for every route)
- College student-athlete on a roster, at a school in a state permitting NIL. Verification required in-app.
- Under 18: parental consent. International athletes: visa constraints apply (noted, not a route).
