# Small Deposit Bonuses — Full Research Report (Sept 25, 2026)

## The blunt verdict

The "deposit $10, get $50 cash" unicorn does not exist in 2026 among cleanly withdrawable offers. Best clean multiples: Robinhood 5x (30-day wait), Revolut 5x (geo-dependent), Swan Bitcoin 2x, Venmo/PayPal 2x, Coinbase 1.33x. Everything else is 1x or below, randomized, locked 60–180 days, trading credit, or dead.
