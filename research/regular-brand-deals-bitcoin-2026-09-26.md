# Regular-people brand deals + extractable bitcoin — research 2026-09-26

The NIL Club "Brand Deals tab" model already exists for regular people as UGC/creator marketplaces.
No followers needed on most — brands buy the content, not the audience.
8 new routes (R9331–R9338) + 1 retirement (R0345).

## Creator brand-deal platforms (regular people)

### Billo (billo.app)
- UGC video marketplace; NO follower requirement. 5,000+ vetted creators.
- Creator payouts on-platform: from $30 per 15-sec video (new creators), $70+ (premium); accumulate with length/add-ons (hooks, organic posting, whitelisting).
- Market rates 2026: entry $50–$150/video, experienced $150–$350, premium $350–$500+.
- Payouts every 2 weeks via PayPal. Geo: US, UK, Canada, Australia.
- Honest catch: low-end tasks ($25–45) exist; competitive applications; you don't control where content runs (often paid ads).

### JoinBrands (joinbrands.com)
- 250,000+ creators, no follower minimum, fixed transparent pricing, escrow-protected.
- 4 earn modes: (1) UGC content — fixed fee per piece (photos/video: $50–$150+ video, $15–$40 images); (2) TikTok Shop affiliate — flat fee per video PLUS 15–30% commission per sale + performance bonuses, free product kept; (3) influencer posts on own channels; (4) monthly retainers.
- Cash out to bank or PayPal anytime; levels 1–5 unlock premium jobs; certifications earn up to 3x more invites.

### Collabstr (collabstr.com)
- Marketplace: 530,000+ brands, 1.2M influencers; creators list fixed-price packages, brands book directly; payment held until work approved.
- Supports IG/TikTok/YouTube/Twitch/X/Amazon/UGC. Typical bookings $100–$500 (hirevire 2026 comparison).
- Honest catch: you set prices but brands choose — new profiles start slow; niche demand varies.

### Insense (insense.pro)
- UGC + paid-ad whitelisting; negotiated per campaign; wallet to PayPal or bank transfer.
- Honest catch: negotiated (not fixed-price), steeper learning curve.

### Influenster (influenster.com)
- Free VoxBoxes (often full-size products, e.g. $100+ perfume) for honest reviews; profile-matched, no followers needed; must be quick — offers go fast.
- Pays in product, NOT cash.

### BzzAgent (bzzagent.com)
- Free BzzKits (full-size + samples) for reviews; BzzScore rises with activity; surveys match you to campaigns.
- Pays in product, NOT cash.

## Extractable bitcoin (verified withdrawal, not locked points)

### Fold (foldapp.com) — NASDAQ: FLD, publicly traded
- Buy gift cards (Amazon, Apple, Nike, DoorDash, Home Depot, Uber, hundreds of brands) in-app, earn 1–20% back in sats (rate shown pre-purchase).
- Fold debit card: flat 1% sats-back or spin-the-wheel for higher rates; no credit check, no annual fee.
- EXTRACTION: withdraw sats to your own personal Bitcoin wallet (no lock-in; processed weekly). Rewards are real BTC, not points.
- 18+, US. Honest framing: converts existing spend into BTC — doesn't create money from nothing; BTC value is volatile.

### Lolli (lolli.com)
- Browser extension/app: BTC cashback on online shopping at partner retailers; rate varies by retailer (typical low-single digits %, promos higher).
- EXTRACTION: transfer to any bitcoin address OR withdraw to USD bank account once balance ≥ $15. No wallet needed to start (custodial until withdrawal).
- Rewards confirm ~90 days after purchase (retailer return windows). US only.
- Honest framing: same as Fold — monetizes spend you already do.

## Realistic regular-person math (for reply, not catalog)
- UGC side hustle: 3 videos/week at ~$75 avg = ~$900/month once established; starting out expect $30–$75/video and slow bookings.
- TikTok Shop affiliate: 15–30% commissions — scales with sales volume, $0 until you convert.
- Product seeding: $0 cash, but free household/beauty products offset spending.
- BTC stacking on spend: $1,000/mo routed spend at ~2% avg = ~$20/mo in BTC you actually own and can withdraw.
