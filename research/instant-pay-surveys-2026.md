# Instant-Pay Surveys & Market Research — 2026 Same-Day Cash Research
**Research date:** 2026-09-25. Scope: platforms paying CASH (not points/gift cards, or explicitly labeled when points-based) via PayPal/direct deposit **same day or within 24 hours** of completing work.

**Critical rule applied:** five timestamps separated — (1) qualify, (2) complete, (3) researcher approval, (4) withdrawable balance, (5) PayPal/delivery. A platform only qualifies if cash-in-hand lands ≤24h after finishing the work. "Instant credit" or "instant cashout of approved balance" alone does not qualify if approval takes days.

---

## STRICT LIST — cash-denominated, ≤24h cash-in-hand, evidence of 2026 operation

### 1. Surveytime (surveytime.io)
- Per-survey: **$1 flat per completed survey** (testing $0.50 micro-tasks; same for 1-min or 10-min surveys)
- Payout timing: **instant — paid within minutes to a few hours** after each survey; no points system
- Minimum: **none**
- Method: PayPal, bitcoin, gift cards (choice per survey)
- Eligibility: global; phone-number verification required
- 2026 evidence: surveytime.io live (crawled same day as research); Android app at v1.0.168; SurveyPolice 2026 rankings; Trustpilot user reviews describing instant payment
- Caveat: survey availability is the bottleneck — realistic earnings are "beer money"

### 2. Forthright
- Per-survey: cash, **$1–2+ typical; exact dollar value stated in the invite email** (no points system)
- Payout timing: validated members paid **instantly after completing a survey**; 2026 SurveyPolice reviewer confirms "money by PayPal in under a day, literally every time"
- Minimum: **none** (rewards <$10 carry a $0.25 redemption fee)
- Method: PayPal cash, bitcoin via Coinbase, Amazon/Tango cards
- Eligibility: 18+ typically; survey volume is LOW (one reviewer received 3 surveys in a month)
- 2026 evidence: SurveyPolice 2026 reviews; official policy text via SurveyPolice ("validated members will receive their rewards instantly")

### 3. 1Q (app)
- Per-survey: **up to $0.25 per question**
- Payout timing: **instant PayPal per response**
- Minimum: **none**
- Eligibility: app-based; demographic profile + location services increase pay; sporadic question availability
- 2026 evidence: Google Play listing live with 1M+ users — https://play.google.com/store/apps/details/1Q?id=com.oneq.askvert&hl=en_IN

### 4. Five Surveys (fivesurveys)
- Per-survey: **$1 effective ($5 per 5 completed surveys)**, cash, no points
- Payout timing: PayPal redemptions **within ~30 minutes** after reaching threshold
- Minimum: **$5** (one batch of 5 surveys)
- Eligibility: 18+, no teens
- 2026 evidence: paidsurveyupdate comparison; sidehustleseattle tested it for an hour (4 full + 2 half surveys completed)
- Caveat: high screen-out rate reported

### 5. AttaPoll (app)
- Per-survey: cash amounts shown before each survey (cents to a few dollars)
- Payout timing: PayPal reportedly **within minutes**
- Minimum: **~$3**
- Eligibility: 13+ reported; ~70 countries
- 2026 evidence: moneyhackhq.com review (Sept 2026); gatherxp.com roundup
- Caveat: official terms / per-survey range need confirmation from the app

### 6. Qmee
- Per-survey: cash amounts shown per survey (range not yet established)
- Payout timing: **no-minimum PayPal withdrawal, reported ~15 minutes**
- Eligibility: country/age rules not yet established from sources checked
- 2026 evidence: realwaystoearnmoneyonline.com extra-cash-sites-instant-payments roundup
- Caveat: needs per-survey range + official timing confirmation

### 7. ZoomRx — HEALTHCARE PROFESSIONALS ONLY
- Per-survey: **$1–$3 per minute**
- Payout timing: **"immediate" after completion** via PayPal, Zelle, or ACH
- Minimum: none reported
- Eligibility: physicians, residents, pharmacists, dentists, PAs, NPs, nurses (panel-dependent)
- 2026 evidence: modernmeded.com/market-research-surveys/ (secondary — needs official corroboration)

### 8. InCrowd — HEALTHCARE PROFESSIONALS ONLY
- Per-survey: **~£6–£10 per 5-minute micro-survey**, increasing with length
- Payout timing: PayPal **within 24 hours**
- Eligibility: doctors, nurses, PAs, psychologists, pharmacists, dentists, vets
- 2026 evidence: medicfootprints.org (secondary — needs official corroboration)

### 9. Mindswarms
- Per-study: **$10–$50 per approved video study** (catalog record: $50 for a typical 7-question response)
- Payout timing: PayPal **within 24 hours** of approval
- Eligibility: 18+, study-specific qualification, US residency in some entries, PayPal, camera/quiet recording space
- Caveat: approval timing needs verification from Mindswarms' current pages

## SECONDARY LIST — fast PayPal but points-denominated, or strong claims needing verification

### Prime Opinion
- Points (1 pt = $0.01), surveys $0.05–$2.50; $5 min (one tested source found $1 PayPal minimum); payouts "processed within 30 minutes"; official site claims first withdrawal in as little as 17 minutes. Points-based → secondary. 18+. Active 2026.

### Opinion Outpost
- Points; $10 min (100 pts); rewards "often delivered instantly… uncommon to wait more than a few minutes/hours" per SurveyPolice 2026. Points-based → secondary.

### Curious Cat
- Points (20–200/survey; 100 pts = £1); £1 minimum; app description says PayPal redemption is "instant". 16+, not all countries. 2026 activity not proven → secondary/unverified.

### GrabPoints — CONTESTED, do NOT count as verified
- Operator (grabpoints.com) claims "cash out same day / arrived instantly"; independent reviews (MoneyPantry, EarnFromTheWeb) say ~48h processing. Official claim vs independent evidence conflict → fails the strict bar until resolved.

### Freecash
- Surveys/offers with PayPal "in minutes" claimed; first withdrawal reportedly $5–$20 + email/identity verification; rules vary by region. Needs official end-to-end confirmation.

### EasyShift
- WSJ (via etcentric.com) reported same-day payouts ("work during the day, paid that night"); 2026 operating status NOT verified → needs confirmation before use.

### Google Opinion Rewards (iOS)
- $2 PayPal minimum (iOS); surveys $0.10–$1.00. Payout timing to PayPal not established → needs verification.
